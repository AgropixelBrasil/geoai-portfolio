Urban overhead transmission corridor — vegetation mapping

Drone orthomosaic, QGIS geodatabases and a browser-based WebGIS demonstrator for mapping vegetation-maintenance areas along an urban power-line corridor in Ribeirão Preto, Brazil.

Scope and methodology

Interpretation of UAV imagery, digitization of polygons, identification of overhead line axes and right-of-way limits, area computation, geospatial QA and interactive cartographic delivery through QGIS2Web / Leaflet.

Interpretation

The reported 42,473.1 m² represent the mapped target area in the final GIS layer. They are not evidence of vegetation already cut or an electrical-engineering design. The map is a reduced-resolution public portfolio preview.
